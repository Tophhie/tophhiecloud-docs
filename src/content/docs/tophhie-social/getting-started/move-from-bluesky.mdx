If you already have an account on Bluesky's servers, you can move it here instead of
starting again. Migration keeps your DID, so you keep your posts, your followers and
everyone you follow.

## What you need

Your current handle and password. Access to the email address on the account, because
the old server emails you a confirmation token partway through and the migration
cannot finish without it. Your two-factor code, if you have 2FA enabled. And the name
you want here, since your handle becomes `<name>.tophhie.social`.

## Before you start

:::caution
Account migration carries real risk. It rewrites the identity record that points at
your server, and a failed migration can leave an account in a broken state that needs
manual repair. Do not migrate an account you cannot afford to disrupt without reading
the warnings in the tool.
:::

Export your data from Bluesky first if you have anything you would hate to lose. Then
give yourself twenty uninterrupted minutes rather than doing this on a phone on a
train, because you need to be able to reach your email halfway through.

## Migrate with PDS MOOver

Use [PDS MOOver](https://pdsmoover.com/), a well-established third-party migration
tool. It runs entirely in your browser, so your password is not sent to Tophhie Social
or to PDS MOOver's servers.

<Steps>

1. Open [pdsmoover.com](https://pdsmoover.com/) and choose the Moover tool.

2. Enter your current handle and current password.

   The password you enter also becomes the password on your migrated account.

3. Fill in the destination:

   | Field | Value |
   | --- | --- |
   | New PDS URL | `https://pds.tophhie.cloud` |
   | New Handle | `<your-name>.tophhie.social` |
   | New Email | the email address you want on the account here |
   | Invite Code | leave blank, because Tophhie Social does not require one |

4. Read the disclaimer, confirm you understand the risks, and start the migration.

5. When prompted, fetch the PLC token that your old server emails you and enter it.
   This is the step that actually moves your identity across, and it will not proceed
   without the token.

6. Once it reports success, sign in with your new handle. See
   [Choose a client](/tophhie-social/getting-started/choose-a-client/).

</Steps>

## After migrating

<Steps>

1. Check your images came across. Blobs, meaning images and video, sometimes lag
   behind the rest of a migration. PDS MOOver's Missing Blobs tool finds anything that
   did not transfer so you can pull it over.

2. Deactivate the old account. Leaving it active can confuse the network about where
   your repository lives, and PDS MOOver's Turn Off helper handles this.

3. Check your profile and settings. Your profile should have travelled with you.
   Tophhie Social's own preferences are at
   [profile.tophhie.social](https://profile.tophhie.social/).

</Steps>

If you would rather keep your own domain as your handle than take a
`.tophhie.social` one, you can switch after migrating. See
[Use your own domain](/tophhie-social/getting-started/use-your-own-domain/).

## Coming from a third-party server

The same tool and the same destination values apply if you are moving from a PDS other
than Bluesky's. If anything looks unusual, email
[help@tophhie.cloud](mailto:help@tophhie.cloud) before migrating rather than after.

## If it goes wrong

Email [help@tophhie.cloud](mailto:help@tophhie.cloud) with your handle, your DID if
you know it, and what the tool reported. Include the step it failed on, because
recovery differs a lot depending on whether the identity record was updated before the
failure.
