Sign-ups are open and you do not need an invite code.

:::caution[Already on Bluesky?]
Do not sign up here. Creating a new account gives you a new identity and leaves your
posts and followers behind. Use
[Move from Bluesky](/tophhie-social/getting-started/move-from-bluesky/) instead,
which brings across the account you already have.
:::

## What you need

An email address you can receive mail at, because it verifies the account and is
then used to sign you in securely. You also need a username, which becomes your
handle.

## Sign up

<Steps>

1. Go to [signup.tophhie.social](https://signup.tophhie.social/).

2. Choose a username. Whatever you enter becomes your full handle with
   `.tophhie.social` on the end, so entering `alice` gives you
   `alice.tophhie.social`.

3. Enter your email address and a password.

4. Read the [Terms of Service](https://trust.tophhie.cloud/policy/atproto-terms/)
   and [Privacy Policy](https://trust.tophhie.cloud/policy/atproto-privacy-policy/),
   then select **Create account**.

5. Check your inbox for a verification email from `donotreply@tophhie.social` and
   follow it to confirm the address.

</Steps>

The sign-up form is protected by [hCaptcha](https://www.hcaptcha.com/). You will
usually not see a challenge, but it is why the form cannot be driven
programmatically.

## Signing in

Sign-in happens in whichever app you use rather than on this site, so see
[Choose a client](/tophhie-social/getting-started/choose-a-client/) next. You will
need your handle (`alice.tophhie.social`), your password, and the server address
`https://pds.tophhie.cloud`.

### Email codes

Sensitive actions are protected by email-based two-factor authentication. When you
sign in, expect a code by email and enter it in your app. This applies to every
account and is not something you switch on.

:::tip
If a code does not arrive, check spam for mail from `donotreply@tophhie.social`
before contacting support. It is the most common cause.
:::

## About your handle

Your handle is how people find and mention you. It is not your identity: your
account is really identified by a DID, a permanent identifier that stays the same
when your handle changes. That separation is what lets you move servers later
without losing your followers, and it is explained in
[Your account and your data](/tophhie-social/technical/how-it-works/your-account-and-data/).

If you own a domain, you can use that as your handle instead. See
[Use your own domain](/tophhie-social/getting-started/use-your-own-domain/).

## Trouble signing up

Email [help@tophhie.cloud](mailto:help@tophhie.cloud) with the handle you were
trying to create and roughly when you tried.
