These guides take you from nothing to a working account on the AT Protocol network.

## If you are new to the network

1. [Create an account](/tophhie-social/getting-started/create-an-account/) and pick
   your handle.
2. [Choose a client](/tophhie-social/getting-started/choose-a-client/), which is the
   app you actually read and post from.
3. [Your first post](/tophhie-social/getting-started/your-first-post/) covers
   posting, finding people, and the settings worth changing early.

Once you are set up, you can also
[use a domain you own](/tophhie-social/getting-started/use-your-own-domain/) as your
handle instead of a `.tophhie.social` one.

## If you already have a Bluesky account

Go straight to
[Move from Bluesky](/tophhie-social/getting-started/move-from-bluesky/). Migrating
keeps your existing identity, posts and followers, so you do not need to create a
new account and start over.

:::note
There is no separate Tophhie Social app. Tophhie Social hosts your account, and you
read and post through Bluesky's app or another AT Protocol client. That is why
[Choose a client](/tophhie-social/getting-started/choose-a-client/) is a step of its
own.
:::
