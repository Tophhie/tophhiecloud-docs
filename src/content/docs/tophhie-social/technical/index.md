Split by what you are trying to find out.

[How it works](/tophhie-social/technical/how-it-works/) is written for people with an
account: what the AT Protocol is, what a PDS actually does, where your data lives, and
what happens when you leave. Conceptual, with no server administration in it.

[Infrastructure](/tophhie-social/technical/infrastructure/) is written for technical
readers and anyone running something similar. The stack, the request path, TLS, the
anti-abuse controls, and the automation behind the service.

## A note on accuracy

Everything in this section comes from what Tophhie Cloud has published, meaning the
[Tophhie Social blog posts](https://www.tophhie.cloud/tag/tophhie-social/) including
its post-incident reports, and the server's own public endpoints.

Infrastructure changes. If something here contradicts what you observe, the
observation is probably right and this page is probably stale, so please
[open an issue or a pull request](https://github.com/Tophhie/tophhiecloud-docs).
