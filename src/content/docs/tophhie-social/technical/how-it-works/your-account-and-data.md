Your account is two things that are easy to confuse: an identity and a repository.

## Identity: your DID and your handle

Your DID is your real identifier. It is permanent, it is not owned by Tophhie Social,
and it does not change when you rename yourself or move to a different server.
Everything anyone has ever done to you on the network, every follow and like and reply,
points at your DID.

Your handle is a human-readable label that resolves to your DID. Yours is
`something.tophhie.social`, or a domain you own if you have
[set one up](/tophhie-social/getting-started/use-your-own-domain/). Handles are meant to
be changeable. DIDs are not.

That split is what makes the rest work. Because follows point at your DID rather than at
`tophhie.social`, changing where you are hosted does not break them.

:::note
Your DID also records where your repository currently lives. Migrating servers means
updating that record, which is why migration involves a confirmation token emailed to
you rather than being a simple copy. See
[Leaving or moving on](/tophhie-social/technical/how-it-works/leaving-or-moving-on/).
:::

## Repository: your data

Your repository, usually shortened to repo, is everything you have created, stored as
signed records. It is organised into collections, and each collection is named by a
lexicon: a schema identifier saying what kind of thing the records are.

| Collection | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| `app.bsky.feed.post` | Your posts |
| `app.bsky.feed.like` | Your likes |
| `app.bsky.graph.follow` | Accounts you follow |
| `app.bsky.actor.profile` | Your display name, bio and avatar |
| `social.tophhie.profile` | Your Tophhie Social preferences |

Because of this design, different apps can share one account. An app that invents a new
kind of record just writes its own collection into your existing repo, and does not need
its own account system.

It also means server-specific settings are still yours. Tophhie Social's own
preferences for accessibility scoring, homepage visibility and marketing email are
stored under `social.tophhie.profile` in your repository rather than in a separate
database here. The schema is public and browsable at [pdsls.dev](https://pdsls.dev/), so
you can inspect exactly what is stored.

## Blobs: images and video

Records are small and structured. Images and video are blobs, stored separately and
referenced by the records that use them.

This is worth knowing because blobs behave differently from records during a migration.
They are copied separately and can lag behind, which is why
[Move from Bluesky](/tophhie-social/getting-started/move-from-bluesky/) tells you to
check for missing blobs afterwards.

## Inspecting your own account

Your repository is public data, and you can read it without any special tooling.

Your DID, from your handle:

```bash
curl "https://pds.tophhie.cloud/xrpc/com.atproto.identity.resolveHandle?handle=alice.tophhie.social"
```

What collections your repo contains:

```bash
curl "https://pds.tophhie.cloud/xrpc/com.atproto.repo.describeRepo?repo=alice.tophhie.social"
```

For browsing rather than scripting, [pdsls.dev](https://pdsls.dev/) renders any public
repository, including yours.

:::caution
Records in your repository are public, including ones that apps present as though they
were private. Blocks and mutes are records too. Treat anything you put on the network as
readable by anyone.
:::

## What Tophhie Social holds that is not in your repo

Some data necessarily sits outside your repository. Your email address and password,
used to authenticate you and to send security mail from `donotreply@tophhie.social`. And
operational logs, generated by the server and the infrastructure in front of it.

What is retained, and for how long, is governed by the
[Privacy Policy](https://trust.tophhie.cloud/policy/atproto-privacy-policy/) rather than
by this page.
