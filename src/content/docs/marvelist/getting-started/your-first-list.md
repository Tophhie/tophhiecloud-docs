## Create a list

Create a new list and give it a name. You can pick an icon and a colour, which is worth
doing once you have more than a handful: colour is how you tell lists apart at a glance
in the sidebar and in widgets.

Choose whether it is a **task list** or a **notes list**. Task lists hold tickable items;
notes lists hold rich text. See [Notes](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/notes/).

## Add some tasks

Add a few. A task only needs a title to exist, and everything else is optional.

Try these on one of them:

- **Mark it important.** It appears in your Important smart list.
- **Give it a due date.** It appears in Today or Scheduled depending on when.
- **Add a subtask or two.** The task shows progress as you tick them off.

See [Tasks](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/tasks/) for everything a task can hold.

## Look at your smart lists

Open **Today**. The task you dated appears there alongside anything else due, from every
list you have.

That is the shape of using Marvelist day to day: put things in whichever list they belong
to, then work from the smart lists rather than opening each list in turn. See
[Smart lists](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/smart-lists/).
