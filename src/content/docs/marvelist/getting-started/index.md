## The idea in one paragraph

Everything in Marvelist lives in a list. A list holds tasks, or it holds notes, and you
choose which when you create it. Tasks can have due dates, subtasks, tags, comments and
files. Lists can be shared, locked, archived, or turned into a grocery list. Smart lists
gather tasks from everywhere by due date or importance, so you rarely open a list just
to find what is due.

Events are the one thing that is not a task list. They are covered separately in
[Events](/marvelist/events/).
