Marvelist has a companion Apple Watch app, so you can check off tasks and glance at your
day from your wrist.

## The watch app

It installs alongside the iPhone app. You get your lists, the tasks and notes inside them,
and the ability to tick things off or add a task.

It is deliberately not the whole app. Organising lists, planning events and running Focus
plans are better on a phone or in a browser.

## The watch talks to Marvelist itself

The watch is not a mirror of your phone. It has its own connection to Marvelist and
fetches your lists from the server directly, so a task you tick on your wrist is sent
straight to your account rather than queued for the phone to pass on.

What that means in practice:

- **The watch needs its own internet connection**, over Wi-Fi or its own mobile data.
  Being near your phone is not what makes it work.
- **Your phone can be off, or elsewhere**, and the watch still works so long as it is
  online.
- **A watch that is online but out of date** is a refresh problem rather than a
  proximity problem. Opening the app again is the fix, not walking back to your phone.

Your phone does matter for one thing: signing in. The watch app picks up your sign-in from
the iPhone app rather than asking you to type credentials on a watch screen, so install
and sign in on the phone first.

## Complications

Two, both for the rich watch-face slots:

| Complication | Shows | Slots |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Today | Today's tasks | Corner, circular and rectangular |
| Smart Lists | Your smart list counts | Rectangular |

Add one the usual way: edit the watch face, choose a complication slot, and pick
Marvelist. Today is the more useful of the two on a face you actually look at, because it
fits the small circular and corner slots as well as the rectangular one.

## If the watch looks out of date

Check it is online first, since that is what it needs rather than your phone. A watch with
no Wi-Fi and no mobile data of its own cannot reach Marvelist, however close the phone is.

If it is online and still stale, reopening the app fetches everything again. If it has
signed itself out, open Marvelist on your iPhone once and the watch picks the sign-in back
up.
