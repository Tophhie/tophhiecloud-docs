Things the iOS app can do that a browser cannot.

- [Widgets](/marvelist/apple-devices/widgets/) put your day on the Home Screen and Lock
  Screen, and a running Focus block in the Dynamic Island.
- [Siri and Shortcuts](/marvelist/apple-devices/siri-and-shortcuts/) let you add tasks and
  ask questions without opening the app.
- [Apple Watch](/marvelist/apple-devices/apple-watch/) covers the watch app and its two
  complications.
- [Reminders and Calendar](/marvelist/apple-devices/reminders-and-calendar/) covers
  migrating from iOS Reminders and adding an event to your calendar.

Everything else in these docs works on both iOS and the web.

## Why these matter

Most of what is here is about the gap between having a thought and getting it into
Marvelist. Opening an app, finding a list and typing takes long enough that things get
forgotten. A widget or a Siri phrase closes that gap.
