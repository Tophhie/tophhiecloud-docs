The core of Marvelist. Everything lives in a list, and a list holds either tasks or
notes.

## The basics

- [Lists](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/lists/) covers creating, organising and archiving.
- [List groups](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/groups/) covers folders on the Lists screen.
- [Tasks](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/tasks/) covers due dates, subtasks, importance and
  assignment.
- [Recurring tasks](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/recurring-tasks/) covers things that come
  back.
- [Tags, comments and attachments](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/tags-comments-and-attachments/)
  covers the extras a task can carry.

## Kinds of list

- [Notes](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/notes/) covers rich-text lists.
- [Grocery lists](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/grocery-lists/) covers shopping, grouped by
  aisle.
- [Smart lists](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/smart-lists/) covers All, Today, Scheduled,
  Important and Assigned.

## Keeping lists tidy and private

- [Archiving and tidying up](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/archiving-and-tidying-up/) covers
  clearing out completed work.
- [Change history](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/change-history/) covers seeing what changed
  and who changed it.
- [Locked lists](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/locked-lists/) covers putting a PIN or
  biometric in front of a list.
- [Vaults](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/vaults/) covers encrypting a list's contents.

:::note
Locking and Vault protection are different things and can be used separately. Locking
controls who can open a list on a device. Vault protection encrypts what is in it. The
two pages explain when each is the one you want.
:::
