Smart lists gather tasks from across all your lists, so you can work from one place
rather than opening each list in turn. They sit at the top of the Lists screen, each
showing a count of what is in it.

| Smart list | What it gathers |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Every incomplete task you have, across every list |
| **Today** | Everything due today, plus anything overdue |
| **Scheduled** | Everything with a future due date |
| **Important** | Everything you have flagged as important |
| **Assigned** | Everything assigned to you on a shared list |

They are built automatically. There is nothing to configure and nothing to add to them:
a task appears because of its due date, its flag or its assignment.

## Working from Today

Today is the one most people live in. It holds what is due today and anything overdue,
which means it stays honest: an overdue task does not quietly disappear the next morning.

If Today is permanently overwhelming, the problem is usually due dates being used as
"soon" rather than "actually due". Moving those to Important, which has no date, tends to
fix it.

## Search

Search looks across your lists when you want something specific rather than something
due. It is the right tool for "where did I put that", where smart lists are the right
tool for "what should I be doing".
