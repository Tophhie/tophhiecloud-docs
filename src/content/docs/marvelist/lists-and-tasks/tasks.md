A task only needs a title. Everything else is optional, and most tasks never need more
than a title and a due date.

## Completing and importance

Tick a task to complete it. Mark it **important** and it appears in your Important
[smart list](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/smart-lists/) alongside everything else you have
flagged.

Importance is a flag rather than a scale. It is for the handful of things that genuinely
matter today, and it stops being useful the moment everything is important.

## Due dates

Give a task a due date and it appears in **Today** on the day, and in **Scheduled**
before then. Overdue tasks stay in Today until you deal with them.

Due dates are also what drives reminders and what the
[Today widget](/marvelist/apple-devices/widgets/) shows.

## Detail

Tasks have a detail field for anything that does not fit in the title: context, a link,
what "done" actually means.

## Subtasks and progress

Break a task into subtasks and it tracks progress as you tick them off, so a big task
shows how far along it is rather than sitting stubbornly incomplete.

Use subtasks when the steps only make sense together. If the steps are independent, they
are probably tasks of their own.

## Assignment

On a [shared list](/marvelist/sharing/), a task can be assigned to a member. Assigned
tasks show up in that person's **Assigned** smart list, which is how work gets picked up
without anyone having to ask.
