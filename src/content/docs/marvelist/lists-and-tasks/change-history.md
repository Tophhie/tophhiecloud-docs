Every task keeps a history: each edit, completion and change, newest first and grouped by
day. Open a task and tap the history button in its toolbar.

Each entry reads as a plain sentence, such as "Chris completed this task", with the
specific field changes shown underneath. Your own changes read as "You". A task nobody has
touched since you made it shows an empty history rather than an error.

## Where it earns its place

On a [shared list](/marvelist/sharing/), when something has moved and nobody remembers
moving it. "Who changed the date?" is a question that used to end in a conversation and now
ends in a screen.

Non-human changes are labelled and kept distinct from each other: **Marvelist AI** for the
built-in assistant, **a connected app** for anything acting through the
[AI connector](/marvelist/marvelist-ai/connecting-ai-apps/), and **Marvelist** for the
system itself. So an edit you do not recognise can be traced to the right place rather
than blamed on a person.

## What is free and what is Premium

| | |
| --- | --- |
| A single task's history | Free |
| A whole list's activity feed, from the list menu | [Premium](/marvelist/premium/) |
| Your own activity across everything, from your profile | Premium |

Per-object history is free for everyone. The two feeds that aggregate across many objects
are the Premium part.

## Vault lists are redacted

History on a [Vault-protected list](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/vaults/) is recorded but
redacted. You still see that something happened and who did it, marked **Hidden (Vault
list)**, without the contents.

That is consistent with what Vault protection is for. The fact of a change is not sensitive;
quoting the changed text in a history entry would undo the encryption you turned on.
