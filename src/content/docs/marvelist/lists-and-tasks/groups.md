A group is a named folder on the Lists screen holding as many lists as you like. Groups
have their own icon and colour, and the lists inside them carry a matching accent rail, so
you can see at a glance which lists belong together.

Groups start collapsed, which is the point: a screen of five folders is navigable where a
screen of twenty lists is not.

## Creating and filling one

Create a group from the Lists screen, then drag lists into it. Rename or delete a group at
any time from the menu beside it.

Lists that are not in a group appear under **Ungrouped** at the bottom. There is no
obligation to file everything; a list you open several times a day is often better left
where you can reach it without expanding anything.

## Deleting a group

Deleting a group does not delete the lists inside it. They return to Ungrouped.

## Everything syncs

Groups sync across all your devices and work offline. Changes made without a connection
are queued and applied when you are back.

## How many to have

Fewer than you think. Groups are worth adding when the Lists screen has become something
you scroll rather than scan, and the useful number is usually four or five: enough to
separate work from home from a project, few enough that you never wonder which folder
something went in.

If you find yourself opening three folders to find one list, the grouping is wrong rather
than incomplete.
