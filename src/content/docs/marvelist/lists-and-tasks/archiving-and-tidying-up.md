## Tidy up completed tasks

Bulk tidy-up removes completed tasks older than a number of days you choose, so a
long-running list does not accumulate hundreds of ticked-off items.

Choose the age rather than clearing everything. Keeping the last week or two means you can
still see what you finished recently, which is useful for a weekly review and for
remembering whether you actually did something.

:::caution
Tidying up deletes those tasks. It is not archiving, and they do not go anywhere you can
retrieve them from. Pick the number of days deliberately.
:::

There is also a Siri shortcut for tidying a list on iOS. See
[Siri and Shortcuts](/marvelist/apple-devices/siri-and-shortcuts/).

## Archiving a list

Archiving takes a whole list out of your sidebar without deleting it. Use it for finished
projects and anything seasonal.

Archived lists live in their own section, and can be **restored** back to the sidebar or
**deleted** permanently from there.

## Archive rather than delete

Archiving is reversible; deleting is not. Unless you are certain, archive.

Archived lists still count towards your storage if they hold
[attachments](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/tags-comments-and-attachments/#attachments), so
if you are archiving to free up space, that will not do it. Delete the attachments, or the
list, instead.
