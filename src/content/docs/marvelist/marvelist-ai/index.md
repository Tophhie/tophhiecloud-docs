Marvelist AI answers questions about your own tasks, notes and events, and helps you act on
them. It is powered by Claude.

- [Chat and insights](/marvelist/marvelist-ai/chat-and-insights/) covers asking it things.
- [Connecting other AI apps](/marvelist/marvelist-ai/connecting-ai-apps/) covers using
  Marvelist from Claude and other AI clients.

Marvelist AI is a [Premium](/marvelist/premium/) feature.

## What it is for

Questions you could answer yourself by opening four lists and reading them:

- "What's due this week?"
- "What do I need to do before the launch party?"
- "Plan my Saturday."

And turning a brain-dump into an organised list, which is the thing it saves the most time
on.

## What it can reach

Your tasks, notes and events. Not [Vault-protected lists](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/vaults/),
which are encrypted and kept from the assistant entirely.

## Privacy

Marvelist's published position is that the assistant only sees the data needed to answer
your question, and that your content is never used to train models.

Tophhie Cloud's [Responsible AI Policy](https://trust.tophhie.cloud/policy/responsible-ai-policy) covers the wider
position, including the parts worth knowing: AI interactions may be logged for quality,
security and reliability, and AI output is assistive rather than authoritative, so you are
expected to check anything you act on.
