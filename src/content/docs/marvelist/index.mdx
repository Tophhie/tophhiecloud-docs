| --- | --- |

## Free and Premium

The core of Marvelist is free forever, and that includes the things most apps charge
for: unlimited lists, tasks and notes, sharing and assignment, smart lists, grocery
lists, and sync across every device.

Premium adds Marvelist AI, unlimited events, 20 GB of cloud storage instead of 1 GB,
unlimited Vaults, and Focus statistics. One subscription covers both web and iOS. See
[Premium](/marvelist/premium/).

## Everything syncs, including offline

Start a list on your phone, finish it on the web, tick it off on your watch.

Both the apps and the web work offline. Changes you make without a connection are held
and sent when you are back, so a train tunnel does not lose your work. See
[Apps and devices](/marvelist/getting-started/apps-and-devices/#working-offline).
