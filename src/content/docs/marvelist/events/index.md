Events are Marvelist's answer to planning something with people: a guest list, RSVPs, and
a public page guests can respond on without downloading anything or creating an account.

- [Creating an event](/marvelist/events/creating-an-event/) covers the basics.
- [Guests and households](/marvelist/events/guests-and-households/) covers adding people,
  plus-ones and categories.
- [Event parts](/marvelist/events/event-parts/) covers events with several parts, such as
  a ceremony and a reception, each with its own RSVP.
- [Schedule](/marvelist/events/schedule/) covers the run-of-show every guest sees.
- [Questions and responses](/marvelist/events/questions-and-responses/) covers asking
  guests things and reading the answers.
- [Invites and RSVP links](/marvelist/events/invites-and-rsvp-links/) covers getting the
  invitation out and tracking who has replied.
- [Co-organisers and lists](/marvelist/events/co-organisers-and-lists/) covers sharing the
  organising.

## What guests see

A public RSVP page. They open a link, respond, answer any questions you asked, and that is
it. No app, no account.

You see attendance counts, plus-ones and answers update live.

## Free and Premium

The free plan has two limits. [Premium](/marvelist/premium/) removes both.

| | Free | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Events | 2 | Unlimited |
| Guests per event | 50 | Unlimited |

The Events screen shows how many of your two you have used.

The limit counts events **you own**, including
[archived](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/archiving-and-tidying-up/) ones. Events other people
have shared with you do not use up your two.

:::caution[Archiving does not free a slot]
Archiving last year's party leaves it counting against your two. To get the slot back you
have to delete the event.
:::

Everything inside an event is the same on both plans. Two free events are fully featured
rather than cut-down, so the free plan covers a wedding or a party comfortably. It is
running several at once, or a guest list past fifty, that needs Premium.

## The limit follows the event's owner

Both limits are checked against the subscription of whoever **owns** the event, not
whoever is adding to it.

So a co-organiser on somebody else's Premium event can add guests past fifty, because the
owner's Premium applies. And if you own the event on the free plan, your
[co-organisers](/marvelist/events/co-organisers-and-lists/) hit your limit at fifty however
many of them have Premium themselves.

If a guest will not save on a shared event, this is usually why, and it is the event's
owner who needs to upgrade.
