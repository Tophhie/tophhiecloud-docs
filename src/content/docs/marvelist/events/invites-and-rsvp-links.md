## Email invites

Send invitations by email, individually or in bulk. Each guest gets a link that identifies
them, so replies come back attached to the right person and households reply once for
everyone.

## The public RSVP link

Every event also has a **public RSVP link** that anyone can use, which is the one to put in
a group chat or on a card. It can be switched off entirely from the event screen, which
closes the public page without affecting individual invitations.

Guests do not need a Marvelist account and do not need to install anything. They open the
link in a browser, find their own name, respond for themselves and their party, and answer
your questions.

## Styling the RSVP page

**Manage Event → Customise RSVP Page** sets the background and accent colour guests see.
Six presets are built in:

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marvelist | Midnight | Ivory & Gold |
| Blush | Sage | Slate |

You can also set custom colours if none of them suit.

Keep contrast in mind rather than picking on the swatch alone. Guests open these on phones,
outdoors, often in a hurry, and a pale accent on a pale background is the one thing that
stops people finding the button.

Removing the Marvelist logo from the page is a [Premium](/marvelist/premium/) feature. The
theme itself is available on both plans.

## Regenerating the link

The public link can be regenerated. Doing so invalidates the old one, which is what you want
if a link has gone somewhere it should not have.

Anyone who already replied keeps their response; only the link changes.

## Tracking replies

The RSVP summary shows attending, declined, pending and plus-ones. With
[event parts](/marvelist/events/event-parts/) you get headcounts per part as well.

Pending is the number that matters as the date approaches, because it is your chase list.

## Resetting RSVPs

You can reset RSVPs for the event, which puts everyone back to pending.

It clears each guest's status, their plus-one numbers, their dietary requirements, their
answers to your questions and their per-part responses. It keeps the guests themselves,
their [event part](/marvelist/events/event-parts/) entitlements, and their links, so
nobody needs re-inviting and no link stops working.

:::caution
Resetting clears replies you have already collected, and they do not come back. It is for a
genuine restart, such as a change of date that invalidates every previous answer, not for
tidying up.

Export the guest list first if there is any chance you will want the old answers. See
[Guests and households](/marvelist/events/guests-and-households/#exporting-the-list).
:::
