Where you subscribed determines where you manage it. This is the thing that confuses people
most, so it is worth checking before you go looking.

## If you subscribed on the web

Billing runs through Stripe. Subscribe from the Premium page in the web app, and manage it
from the billing portal linked there.

The portal is where you update payment details, switch between monthly and annual, and
cancel.

## If you subscribed on iOS

The subscription belongs to your Apple account, and Marvelist cannot change it. Manage it in
**Settings → your name → Subscriptions** on your device.

That is where you cancel, change plan or update payment details. Cancelling in the App Store
is the only way to cancel an App Store subscription.

## Cancelling

Cancel from wherever you subscribed. Premium continues until the end of the period you have
paid for, then the account returns to the free plan.

Nothing is deleted when Premium ends. Your lists, tasks, notes and events stay. What changes
is that the Premium features stop: no AI, and the limits come back.

If you are over the free storage limit when Premium ends, existing attachments are still
there; you will need to clear space before adding more. See
[Cloud storage](/marvelist/account/cloud-storage/).

## Complimentary Premium

Some accounts have Premium provided at no cost. If yours does, the Premium page says so, and
there is nothing to pay or manage.

## Refunds

Refunds are covered by the Marvelist refund policy at
[trust.tophhie.cloud](https://trust.tophhie.cloud/). App Store purchases are refunded by
Apple rather than by Marvelist.

## Deleting your account while subscribed

You cannot delete your account while a paid subscription is active. Cancel first, then
delete. See [Deleting your account](/marvelist/account/deleting-your-account/).
