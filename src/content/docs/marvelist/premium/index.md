limits, and gives you considerably more storage.

## Price

**£2.99 a month**, or **£34.99 a year**.

One subscription covers both web and iOS. Subscribing on one gives you Premium on the other,
because it is the same account.

## What is genuinely free

Worth being clear, because plenty of apps charge for these: unlimited lists, unlimited
tasks, unlimited notes, sharing with as many people as you like, task assignment, smart
lists, grocery lists, and sync across every device.

You are not on a trial that expires, and the free plan is not crippled.

## Deciding whether to upgrade

The three reasons people actually upgrade:

- **You want the AI**, either inside Marvelist or connected to the AI app you already use.
- **You are hosting something**, and either you need a third event or the guest list has
  gone past fifty. A wedding is the usual trigger.
- **You attach a lot of files**, and 1 GB has run out.

If none of those apply, the free plan is genuinely fine.
