## Profile

Edit your name and add a profile photo from **Profile** in the app. Your photo is what
other people see on [shared lists](/marvelist/sharing/), in comments, and in the presence
avatars showing who is currently viewing a list.

Worth setting if you share lists with anyone. It is much easier to tell who assigned what
when people have faces.

The Profile screen also shows how long you have had the account, your
[storage](/marvelist/account/cloud-storage/) usage, your
[Premium](/marvelist/premium/) status and your unique user ID. Support may ask for that ID;
nothing else needs it.

## Your email address cannot be changed

Your email address is your Tophhie Cloud sign-in identity, and it is fixed. Marvelist
cannot change it, and there is no longer a "change email" flow in the app.

If you have an older version of Marvelist that still shows one, it will decline with a
message saying the address is managed by your Tophhie Cloud sign-in.

This is a consequence of how sign-in works now. The address is the credential you
authenticate with rather than a contact detail Marvelist owns, and the copy Marvelist keeps
is only a mirror of it, used for notifications and for showing you who you are signed in
as.

If you genuinely need the address on your account changed, contact support rather than
looking for a setting.

## Your name

Your name comes from the same place. Change it in your Tophhie Cloud account via **Profile
→ Manage my account**; see [Tophhie Cloud Account](/account/).

## Older Apple, Google and Microsoft accounts

Sign-in with Apple, Google and Microsoft is legacy. If your account predates Tophhie Cloud
accounts it may still carry one of those identities, but new sign-ins use Tophhie Cloud and
that is where your details are managed.
