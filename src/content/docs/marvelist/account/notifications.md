**Settings → Notifications** controls what Marvelist notifies you about. Each is a separate
switch, so you can keep the ones that matter without taking all of them.

| Notify me when | Useful because |
| --- | --- |
| A list is shared with me | You would otherwise not know it had arrived |
| A shared task is assigned to me | Someone has given you something to do |
| A new entry has been added to a shared list | Keeps you current on an active shared list |
| Someone comments on a task | Comments are conversations, and conversations expect replies |

Every one of these is about shared lists. Nothing here notifies you about your own tasks
on your own lists.

## Which to keep on

Assignment and comments are the two worth keeping. Both mean somebody is waiting on you,
which is exactly the case a notification is for.

"A new entry has been added to a shared list" is the one to turn off first. On an active
list it fires constantly and stops meaning anything, which then trains you to ignore the
other three.

## Due dates and reminders

Those are separate from this screen. A task notifies you based on its own due date; see
[Tasks](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/tasks/).

[Focus](/marvelist/focus/pomodoro/) also notifies you at the end of every block, focus and
break alike, so you can put the phone down while a block runs.

## The notification feed

The bell at the top of the app holds the same notifications as a list, so something
dismissed on the Lock Screen is not lost. Siri can read it back with the **Notification
Feed** shortcut. See [Siri and Shortcuts](/marvelist/apple-devices/siri-and-shortcuts/).

## If nothing is arriving

Check iOS Settings first: Marvelist needs notification permission at the system level
before any of these switches do anything. After that, see
[Troubleshooting](/marvelist/account/troubleshooting/).
