## Inviting someone

Share the list with another Marvelist user and they get access to it. They see it in their
own sidebar alongside their own lists.

## Join links

A list can have a shareable **join link**. Anyone with a Marvelist account who opens it can
join the list, which is easier than inviting people one at a time.

:::caution
Anyone with the link and an account can join. Treat a join link like the contents of the
list: if you would not post the list publicly, do not post the link publicly.
:::

## Read-only access

Members can be made read-only. They see the list and everything on it but cannot change
anything.

This is the right setting for sharing a plan with people who need to know rather than to
help. It keeps a shared itinerary or schedule from drifting because someone tidied it.

## Managing members

**Manage Access** on the list menu shows who has access and lets you change or remove it.
Removing someone takes the list out of their sidebar.

## The sharing controls, and which to use

| Menu item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Share | Share the list with a specific person |
| Manage Access | See and change who currently has access |
| Sharing Link | Create or revoke the join link |
| Share access link | Send that link on |
| Copy Link | Copy it to the clipboard |

Prefer Share for people you know. A join link is for the case where you do not want to add
fifteen people by hand, and it stays live until you revoke it, so revoke it once everyone
is in.

## Clearing up everything at once

If you have shared a lot over the years and lost track, **Settings → Privacy** has
**Invalidate All List Sharing Links** and **Revoke Access to All Shared Lists**. Both apply
across every list you own. See [Security](/marvelist/account/security/).

## What members can see

Everything on the list: tasks, detail, subtasks, tags, comments and attachments. There is
no per-task privacy within a shared list, so anything you would not want a member to read
belongs on a different list.

Locking and Vault protection are about your own devices and storage rather than about
hiding content from members. See [Locked lists](/marvelist/lists-and-tasks/locked-lists/).
