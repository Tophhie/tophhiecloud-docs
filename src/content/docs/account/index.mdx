**[myaccount.tophhie.cloud](https://myaccount.tophhie.cloud)** is where you manage your
Tophhie Cloud account. Update your details, change your password, add or remove ways of
signing in, check where your account has been used, and see which apps you have.

It works with the account you already use to sign in to Tophhie Cloud services. There is
nothing extra to set up and no separate password.

## Why this exists

Microsoft does not provide a "My Account" page for the kind of account Tophhie Cloud
uses, so this portal fills that gap. Your account lives in Microsoft Entra External ID,
and the portal talks to it on your behalf.

That has one practical consequence worth knowing: some of what you see here comes
straight from Microsoft, so a change can take a moment to show up, and sign-in activity
in particular lags by a few hours.

## Making it yours

The portal is available in English, French, German and Spanish, and follows your device
between light and dark appearance. Both settings are in the sidebar, and neither changes
anything about your account itself.

Your preferred language on the [Profile](/account/guides/your-profile/) page is a
separate setting. That one tells Tophhie Cloud services which language to use when they
contact you.

## If something is wrong

Email [help@tophhie.cloud](mailto:help@tophhie.cloud). If you think someone else has
been using your account, change your password first and then
[sign out everywhere](/account/guides/password-and-sign-in-methods/#signing-out-everywhere),
which ends every other session immediately.
