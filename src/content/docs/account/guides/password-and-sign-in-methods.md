Everything here is on the **Security** page.

## Changing your password

Enter your current password, then your new one twice, and save.

Your new password has to meet Tophhie Cloud's requirements. If it does not, you are told
which rule it failed rather than just being refused.

:::note
This only applies if you sign in with an email address and password. If you sign in with
Apple, Google or Microsoft, there is no Tophhie Cloud password on your account, and you
change that password with the provider instead.
:::

You may be asked to confirm your identity partway through, particularly if you have not
signed in recently. That is expected. Changing a password is exactly the action worth
protecting.

## Sign-in methods

**Sign-in methods** lists the ways you can currently get into your account, and lets you
add or remove some of them.

Adding an email method sends a code to that address to confirm you own it. Enter the
code and the method is added.

Removing one is immediate, so check what you are removing before you confirm.

:::caution
Do not remove your last remaining sign-in method. If you do, you will not be able to get
back in, and recovering the account means contacting support.
:::

Sign-in methods are also what
[multi-factor authentication](/account/guides/multi-factor-authentication/) uses to
reach you, so keeping a second one on the account is worth doing even if you never use
it to sign in.

## Signing out everywhere

**Sign out everywhere** ends every session on every device, including the one you are
using.

Use it if you have signed in on a device you no longer have, if you have lent someone a
device, or if you think somebody else has been in your account. Change your password
first in that last case, otherwise whoever it is can simply sign back in.

You will need to sign in again afterwards. Sessions on other devices can take a short
while to actually stop working.

## Recent sign-in

The Security page also shows your most recent sign-in, as a quick check that nothing
looks wrong. The full list is on the
[Sign-in activity](/account/guides/sign-in-activity/) page.
