The **Profile** page holds the details Tophhie Cloud services use to address you.

## What you can change

| Field | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Display name | How your name appears across Tophhie Cloud |
| First name, Last name | Your name in more formal contexts |
| Job title | Optional |
| Street address, City, State or county, Postal code, Country | Optional |
| Preferred language | Which language Tophhie Cloud uses when contacting you |

Edit the fields you want to change and select **Save changes**. **Reset** puts back what
was there before you started editing, as long as you have not saved yet.

Everything except your display name is optional. Leaving a field empty is fine.

:::note
Preferred language is not the same as the language switcher in the sidebar. The
switcher changes the portal you are looking at right now. Preferred language tells
Tophhie Cloud services which language to use in emails and elsewhere.
:::

## What you cannot change here

**Your email address** cannot be changed in the portal. Email
[help@tophhie.cloud](mailto:help@tophhie.cloud) if it needs to change.

**Your password** is on the
[Security](/account/guides/password-and-sign-in-methods/) page rather than here.

**Social sign-ins** such as Apple, Google and Microsoft are managed from the Tophhie
Cloud sign-in page, not the portal. The Profile page lists them so you can see what is
connected. See [Connected identities](/account/guides/your-data/#connected-identities).

## Your account card

At the top of the page, alongside your details, is a card showing your account status
and the date you joined. It is there for reference; there is nothing to change.

## If saving fails

Two messages are worth telling apart.

**"We couldn't verify your identity to save changes."** Your session needs refreshing.
Wait a moment and try again, or sign out and back in.

**"Could not save your profile."** The change did not reach Microsoft. Try again, and
if it keeps happening email [help@tophhie.cloud](mailto:help@tophhie.cloud).

Either way, nothing is saved unless you see the confirmation, so a failed save has not
left your profile half-changed.
