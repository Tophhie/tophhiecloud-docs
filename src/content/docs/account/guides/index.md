Each guide covers one part of the portal. None of them depend on the others, so read
whichever matches what you are trying to do.

## Getting in

- [Signing in](/account/guides/signing-in/) covers reaching the portal and what happens
  when you do.

## Your details

- [Your profile](/account/guides/your-profile/) covers your name, job title, address and
  preferred language.
- [Your data](/account/guides/your-data/) covers downloading a copy of what the account
  holds, and what happens if you want it deleted.

## Security

- [Password and sign-in methods](/account/guides/password-and-sign-in-methods/) covers
  changing your password, adding or removing ways to sign in, and signing out
  everywhere.
- [Multi-factor authentication](/account/guides/multi-factor-authentication/) covers
  turning extra sign-in security on.
- [Sign-in activity](/account/guides/sign-in-activity/) covers checking where and when
  your account has been used.

## Everything else

- [Apps and services](/account/guides/apps-and-services/) covers the Tophhie Cloud apps
  connected to your account.

## In a hurry

If you think your account has been compromised, do these two things in order:

1. [Change your password](/account/guides/password-and-sign-in-methods/#changing-your-password).
2. [Sign out everywhere](/account/guides/password-and-sign-in-methods/#signing-out-everywhere).

Then email [help@tophhie.cloud](mailto:help@tophhie.cloud).
