Go to **[myaccount.tophhie.cloud](https://myaccount.tophhie.cloud)** and select
**Sign in**.

You are sent to the Tophhie Cloud sign-in page, which is the same one you use for every
other Tophhie Cloud service, and returned to the portal once you are through. Whatever
you normally sign in with works here: email and password, or a social account such as
Apple, Google or Microsoft.

There is no separate account or password for the portal.

## Finding your way around

| Page | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | A summary, with shortcuts to the rest |
| Profile | Your name, job title, address and preferred language |
| Security | Password, sign-in methods, multi-factor authentication, sessions |
| Apps and services | Tophhie Cloud apps connected to your account |
| Sign-in activity | Where and when your account has been used |

Language and appearance are in the sidebar as well. They change the portal only, not
your account.

## Signing out

**Sign out** in the sidebar ends your session in this browser.

If you want to end sessions on other devices too, that is a different thing: see
[signing out everywhere](/account/guides/password-and-sign-in-methods/#signing-out-everywhere).

## If you cannot sign in

**You have forgotten your password.** Use the reset link on the sign-in page. The portal
cannot reset it for you, because you have to be signed in to reach it.

**You signed up with Apple, Google or Microsoft.** There is no Tophhie Cloud password on
your account to reset. Sign in with that provider instead. Your
[connected identities](/account/guides/your-data/#connected-identities) on the Profile
page show which ones your account has.

**Sign-in fails or loops.** Try a private browsing window first, which rules out a stale
cookie. If it still fails, email
[help@tophhie.cloud](mailto:help@tophhie.cloud) and say what you were signing in with.
