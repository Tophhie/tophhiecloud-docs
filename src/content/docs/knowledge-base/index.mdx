This is the site you are on. It documents every Tophhie Cloud product in one place, with
one search box across all of them, and it is open source: anything wrong on any page can be
fixed by whoever spots it.

Documenting itself is not a joke at its own expense. The conventions below are the reason
ten product sections look like one site rather than ten, and they only hold if they are
written down somewhere a contributor will find them.

## How it is put together

Each product owns a folder under `src/content/docs/`, and readers only ever see the sidebar
for the product they are in. Search deliberately works the other way round and covers
everything at once, because people rarely know which product owns their answer before they
look for it.

That split is the central idea of the site. [How it works](/knowledge-base/how-it-works/)
covers the mechanics.

## One list, not several

Products are declared once, in `src/data/products.mjs`. The sidebar, the header switcher and
the landing page cards are all built from it.

They used to be maintained separately and drifted apart within a day, which is the entire
argument for the current arrangement. Adding a product means adding an entry there and
creating its folder, and everything else follows.
